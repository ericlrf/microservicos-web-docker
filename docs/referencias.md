# Referências tecnicas

Consulta: 14 de outubro de 2026. O código e a organizacao didática foram adaptados do material fornecido na conversa.

1. Docker. Networking in Compose. https://docs.docker.com/compose/how-tos/networking/
2. Auth0. node-jsonwebtoken: assinatura e verificação, incluindo `algorithms`, `issuer` e `audience`. https://github.com/auth0/node-jsonwebtoken
3. Microsoft. Microservices architecture style. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/guide/architecture-styles/microservices
4. Express. Using middleware. https://expressjs.com/en/guide/using-middleware/
5. http-party. node-http-proxy: fabrica de proxies e preservacao do corpo da requisição. https://github.com/http-party/node-http-proxy
6. Docker. Control startup and shutdown order in Compose. https://docs.docker.com/compose/how-tos/startup-order/
7. Marp Team. Marp CLI: exportacao PPTX e opção experimental `--pptx-editable`. https://github.com/marp-team/marp-cli
8. Node.js. End-of-Life e Node.js 24 LTS. https://nodejs.org/en/about/eol e https://nodejs.org/pt-br/blog/release/v24.11.0
9. npm. `npm ci`. https://docs.npmjs.com/cli/v11/commands/npm-ci/
10. Docker. Docker Compose overview e `up`. https://docs.docker.com/compose/ e https://docs.docker.com/reference/cli/docker/compose/up/
11. Docker. Version and name top-level elements. https://docs.docker.com/reference/compose-file/version-and-name/
12. Docker. Services: `container_name`, saúde e reinicio. https://docs.docker.com/reference/compose-file/services/
13. npm registry. Metadados de Express 5.3.0 consultados em `latest`. https://registry.npmjs.org/express/latest

As notas dos slides também incluem blocos `[Sources]`. As fontes não substituem a validação do projeto no computador da aula.
